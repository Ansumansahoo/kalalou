Forty Years of Sourcing

We travel the lesser-known corners of the world to find craftsmen making beautiful, honest, sustainable things.

Kalalou is a family-owned wholesale home décor company based in Jackson, Mississippi. Since 1986 we have built long-term partnerships with artisans across India, Vietnam, the Philippines, and beyond — using recycled metal, sustainably sourced wood, and hand-loomed textiles.

Our line of roughly 1,500 SKUs spans furniture, lighting, garden décor, holiday, and gift. We are proud to be the 2025 ARTS Award winner for Home Accents Vendor of the Year.

Faux Concrete Sheep Planter

Crafted in a faux concrete finish, this sheep planter brings a calm, rustic charm to any space. The sculpted texture and soft, weathered tones give it the look of a piece discovered in a cottage garden. A roomy planting area makes it easy to fill with greenery or seasonal stems. Finished on all sides with a solid bottom and no drain hole, it is designed for effortless styling indoors or in covered outdoor spaces.
